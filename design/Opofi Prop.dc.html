Daily loss limit
You may not lose more than 5% of the account's starting balance in one day, counting open and closed trades. The day runs from 00:00 to 23:59 server time.
Example · On a $100K account your daily loss limit is $5,000. If your equity falls to $95,000 at any point during the day, the account is breached. The limit resets at 00:00 server time.

Questions
What happens at 00:00 server time?
Your daily loss allowance resets to 5% of the starting balance. Open positions carry over and count toward the new day.
Is there a time limit on either phase?
No. Trade at your pace; only the minimum of 4 trading days per phase applies.
Do rule updates affect my running account?
No. The version shown at checkout is locked to that account for its whole life.
